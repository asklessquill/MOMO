# MOMO Purpose and Principles

## Purpose

**The whole System exists to convert Human Purpose into meaningful real-world change through the different strengths of independent autonomous Applications.**

> **鬼ヶ島とは現実世界である。**

Architecture, Protocol, AI cooperation and learning serve that Purpose; they are not the final objective. Human is the final source of Purpose and constitutional authority. MOMO preserves, relates, observes and explains Human intent and System-wide philosophy. **MOMO ≠ Human.** MOMO does not become a Supervisor, choose another Application's internal work, or acquire its authority by observing it.

KIBI owns the semantic connection Protocol. Each Application retains its responsibilities, capabilities, internal execution and outward evidence. The System need not have a fixed participant roster or mandatory central runtime.

## MP-01 — Human sovereignty and bounded autonomy

**Human sets Purpose and constitutional boundaries; Applications act autonomously within applicable current authority.**

Human remains the final source of Purpose, value judgments and constitutional additions, changes and retirement. AI may discover, review and propose; it cannot self-ratify. A repository, model, MOMO representation, identity or authentication does not replace Human authority.

Valid delegation permits routine work without repeated Human approval. Action must remain within its Task, purpose, policy, resource, recipient, economic, trust, privacy, security and irreversible-action limits as applicable. Historical permission is not automatically current authority. Completion, cancellation or supersession of a Task does not authorize another Task; continuous activity requires an applicable continuing delegation. Real-world orientation never creates permission to act.

## MP-02 — Independent responsibility and graceful evolution

**Each Application owns its responsibility and internals; cooperation must preserve that ownership through participation, withdrawal, replacement and combination.**

An Application owns its agents, methods of execution, models, retry and recovery, storage and internal state. MOMO, KIBI and other Applications do not take over those internals. Internal complexity remains with its owner; other participants receive sufficient outward meaning rather than a requirement to reconstruct internal work.

Applications may join, decline, leave, be replaced or combine through explicit outward responsibilities and compatible boundaries. No fixed topology or shared implementation is required. Departure may remove a capability; independence does not promise that a missing service continues to exist.

## MP-03 — Complementarity and reusable capability

**Different responsibilities develop different strengths. Make useful capability and learning reusable without silently transferring responsibility.**

> **Role owns responsibility. Capability may be shared.**
>
> **Share capability and learning, not internals.**

The Application exercising a Role remains accountable for that responsibility. Knowledge, judgment, methods, selection criteria, procedures, patterns, success lessons and failure lessons developed through its work should be reusable by other Applications when useful and authorized. Reuse may expose a capability or a deliberately prepared learning artifact through an explicit semantic interface; it need not disclose internal code, task graphs, raw logs, hidden reasoning or private data.

The producing owner states the reusable meaning, scope, evidence, limitations and permitted use. The receiving owner decides applicability and remains accountable for its own use. A shared method or lesson does not grant authority, confer the producer's Role, or require a new central owner. Different strengths may be complementary without being made identical.

> 桃太郎が強いから三匹を連れて行くのではない。
> 三匹には、桃太郎にできないことができる。

## MP-04 — Continuous learning within responsibility

**Each Application should be able to improve itself within its responsibility through Act → Observe → Learn → Improve → Act.**

Learning uses observed outcomes, uncertainty, success and failure to improve subsequent decisions and behavior. It does not require a central Supervisor. The owner remains responsible for judging and verifying improvements before relying on them, within its current Task and authority.

Reusable learning may cross an explicit semantic interface under MP-03. Receiving a lesson does not require adopting it, transferring responsibility or exposing internals. Learning may change a method within delegation; it may not silently rewrite Human Purpose, Principles, another Application's responsibilities, or authority. The cycle stops at the authorized Task boundary unless current delegation explicitly permits continuation.

## MP-05 — Semantic integrity and receiver-level meaning

**Preserve one accountable semantic truth while presenting meaning at the receiver's useful resolution.**

Expand internally as necessary and converge externally to meaningful Results, decisions and limits. Human should not have to reconstruct repository history or internal task graphs to understand the whole. Evidence remains available through authorized drill-down; compression must not turn unknown, partial, rejected or unverified claims into certainty.

> **one semantic truth → multiple views**

Views retain source, revision, scope, provenance and decision status. They do not become competing authorities or silently amend their sources. MOMO's System-wide meaning has one semantic source in MOMO-Observatory; MOMO presents authorized Human-facing projections. Application facts and KIBI Protocol meaning retain their own accountable sources. Observation and synthesis do not confer ownership of those sources.

## MP-06 — AI-native system recomposition

**Use systems for their semantic primitives, not merely their Human-facing screens.**

Use meaningful objects, relations, state, identity, authority, version, native delta, events, audit, evidence and provenance when they provide a more faithful substrate for AI work. Prefer native change over reconstructed context and operate at the smallest meaningful unit, within service, cost, trust, security and recovery constraints.

This is a philosophy of choosing suitable representations, not a permanent requirement to use Git, a SaaS, model, runtime, storage system or UI mechanism. Concrete methods remain replaceable and must preserve Human-usable meaning under MP-05.

## MP-07 — Durable and Safe-to-Ignore operation

**Meaningful state, decisions, authority, evidence and handoff must survive Actor and session changes so Human need not supervise routine work.**

Durability includes a coherent current state, applicable source/adoption status, verified and unresolved claims, outstanding obligations and permitted continuation or stopping conditions. A retained history, successful authentication or saved approval reference alone is insufficient.

Safe to Ignore requires applicable authority, economic and trust limits, privacy and security, evidence, recovery or explicit irreversible limits, and bounded Human attention. Unavailable, blocked, rejected, revoked, expired and uncertain states must remain visible and recoverable in meaning. Hold work whose required authority cannot be established; preserve independent work only within its own active delegation. Routine supervision is unnecessary when these conditions hold, not because the System has become inactive or unaccountable.
