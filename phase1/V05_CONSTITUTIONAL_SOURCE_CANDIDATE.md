# MOMO System v0.5 — Constitutional Source Candidate

Status: **INACTIVE / NONCURRENT / CANDIDATE ONLY**  
Purpose: prepare the constitutional source for the v0.5 responsibility convergence without changing Current authority or runtime behavior.

This candidate does **not** replace `PRINCIPLES.md`, `SOURCE_AND_VIEWS.md`, MOMO-Observatory, or any owning Application source while it remains inactive. It records the already-decided v0.5 destination so later migration can be checked against one explicit target.

## 1. Release intent

v0.5 is a **constitutional and responsibility convergence**, not a capability expansion.

> **能力は増やす。登場人物は増やさない。**

The purpose is to reduce responsibility-bearing identities while preserving useful capability, evidence, provenance and historical recoverability.

A new reusable capability does not require a new Actor. If a genuinely different responsibility world is needed, it belongs in another Momotaro clone/project rather than silently adding another responsibility-bearing Actor to this System.

## 2. Exactly five responsibility-bearing Actors

The v0.5 System has exactly these responsibility-bearing Actors:

| Actor | Constitutional responsibility |
|---|---|
| **MOMO** | System meaning, Human-facing semantic compression, reusable System knowledge, constitutional provenance and views. MOMO is the head of the System, but **MOMO ≠ Human** and **MOMO ≠ Supervisor**. |
| **KIBI** | Semantic connection Protocol between autonomous Applications. KIBI is the nervous system, not an orchestrator and not an owner of Application internals. |
| **KIJI** | World observation, research, interpretation and conversion of evidence into implementable Requirements. |
| **INNU** | Realization of accepted Product/Capability and mastery of implementation/model/tool selection needed to complete that responsibility. |
| **SARU** | Human World Activation, operations and bounded real-world experimentation needed to make capability useful in reality. |

No other repository, runtime, lab, knowledge base, mentor persona, dashboard or tool is a sixth responsibility-bearing Actor under v0.5.

## 3. Responsibility is fixed; capability may move or be reused

The v0.5 identity rule is:

> **Role owns responsibility. Capability may be shared.**

A capability can move, be reused, be exposed through KIBI, or be preserved as knowledge without creating a new constitutional Actor.

Repositories and runtimes may remain technically separate when separation is useful. Technical separation does not imply constitutional independence.

## 4. Ownership convergence target

The following ownership convergence is the v0.5 destination:

| Existing repository / identity | v0.5 responsibility owner | Target meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `MOMO-Observatory` | **MOMO** | Its System semantic-source / observation capability is absorbed into MOMO responsibility. It does not remain a separate responsibility-bearing Actor. |
| `references` | **MOMO** | Reusable, noncanonical knowledge becomes MOMO-owned System knowledge/evidence. Reference remains evidence, not authority. |
| `Dango` | **INNU** | Model/tier routing, prompt compilation and model-selection learning become part of INNU's capability-realization/model-mastery responsibility. |
| `FROG` | **SARU** | Reality Mentor / Leap capability becomes part of SARU's Human World Activation and reality-experimentation responsibility. |
| `SandFrog` | **SARU** | Experimental substrate may remain technically separate/disposable, but its responsibility belongs to SARU. |

The historical repositories remain provenance until separately retired. Routine v0.5 recovery should ultimately not require MOMO-Observatory, references, Dango or FROG as independent current authorities.

## 5. MOMO source responsibility in v0.5

MOMO becomes the accountable System home for:

- Human-ratified System Purpose and Principles;
- System-wide semantic relationships and current integrated interpretation;
- Human-facing views / Cockpit meaning;
- reusable System knowledge and evidence intended for cross-Application use;
- provenance linking current meaning to historical sources and Human decisions.

This does not make MOMO authoritative over another Application's original facts or internals. The owning Application remains authoritative for its own responsibility, implementation and outward facts.

MOMO may represent and relate those facts; it must not silently overwrite them.

## 6. Human authority remains outside the five Actors

Human is not counted as an Actor because Human is the source of Purpose and final constitutional authority, not an Application responsibility inside the System.

Human alone may finally ratify constitutional additions, changes or retirement. AI may recover, interpret, propose, review and implement within delegation; it may not self-ratify constitutional meaning.

MOMO is the closest System representation of Human-approved System meaning, not a replacement Human.

## 7. KIBI remains connection, not control

KIBI owns semantic connection across independent Applications:

- identity and participant meaning;
- versioned semantic Contract;
- scoped/revocable authority context;
- capability/knowledge distinction;
- lifecycle and compatibility meaning;
- durable handoff/provenance;
- negative states and recovery semantics.

KIBI does not choose another Application's internal work, own its task graph, become the central runtime, or become a Supervisor.

## 8. KIJI / INNU / SARU working cycle

The three working responsibilities remain distinct:

**KIJI → Requirements**  
Observe, research, interpret, quantify and reduce uncertainty until the System has evidence-backed, implementable Requirements.

**INNU → Product / Capability**  
Choose and master the necessary implementation/model/tool path, build the accepted Product/Capability and finish it to the required evidence standard.

**SARU → Human World Activation**  
Turn accepted Product/Capability into authorized operation, adoption, experiments and real-world state change, while respecting Human authority and external-mutation boundaries.

They may reuse one another's capability and learning without erasing responsibility boundaries.

## 9. Reference is evidence, not authority

Reusable references, research, model-routing episodes, experiment reports and historical artifacts are evidence inputs.

They do not become constitutional or operational authority merely because they are newer, detailed, machine-readable or stored under MOMO/INNU/SARU.

Every current claim must preserve the distinction among:

- fact;
- observation;
- interpretation;
- representation/view;
- evidence;
- currentness;
- authority;
- history.

## 10. Historical retirement principle

A repository whose responsibility is absorbed may later become historical/read-only/retired, but only after migration and recovery evidence prove that the current System no longer depends on it as a live authority.

Retirement must preserve provenance and accepted historical evidence. Retirement is not deletion of history.

No retirement is activated by this Phase 1 candidate.

## 11. Non-goals for Phase 1

This candidate does not:

- activate v0.5;
- replace current `PRINCIPLES.md` or `SOURCE_AND_VIEWS.md`;
- migrate repository contents;
- change KIBI Protocol implementation;
- change KIJI, INNU or SARU runtime behavior;
- move secrets, credentials or private evidence;
- retire repositories;
- start autonomous cycles;
- authorize external mutation;
- infer Phase 2 authority from Phase 1 completion.

## 12. Activation condition

This document can become part of Current constitutional meaning only after the v0.5 migration program reaches an explicit Human-authorized activation step and the candidate has passed the required semantic/recovery/review gates applicable at that time.

Until then:

> **Current remains v0. Human-approved v0.5 direction is recorded here as an inactive destination, not as already-completed reality.**
