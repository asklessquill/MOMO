# MOMO System v0.5 — Constitutional Source Candidate

Status: **INACTIVE / NONCURRENT / CANDIDATE ONLY**

v0.5 is a constitutional and responsibility convergence, not a capability expansion.

> **能力は増やす。登場人物は増やさない。**

This candidate does not replace current `PRINCIPLES.md`, `SOURCE_AND_VIEWS.md`, MOMO-Observatory, or any owning Application source while inactive.

## Exactly five responsibility-bearing Actors

| Actor | Constitutional responsibility |
|---|---|
| **MOMO** | System meaning, Human-facing semantic compression, reusable System knowledge, constitutional provenance and views. MOMO is the System head, but **MOMO ≠ Human** and **MOMO ≠ Supervisor**. |
| **KIBI** | Semantic connection Protocol between autonomous Applications. KIBI is the nervous system, not an orchestrator and not an owner of Application internals. |
| **KIJI** | World observation, research, interpretation and conversion of evidence into implementable Requirements. |
| **INNU** | Realization of accepted Product/Capability plus implementation/model/tool mastery required to finish that responsibility. |
| **SARU** | Human World Activation, operations and bounded real-world experimentation needed to make capability useful in reality. |

No other repository, runtime, lab, knowledge base, mentor persona, dashboard or tool is a sixth responsibility-bearing Actor under v0.5.

A new reusable capability does not require a new Actor. If a genuinely different responsibility world is needed, it belongs in another Momotaro clone/project rather than silently expanding this Actor set.

## Responsibility and capability

> **Role owns responsibility. Capability may be shared.**

A capability may move, be reused, be exposed through KIBI, or be preserved as knowledge without creating a new constitutional Actor. Technical separation of repositories/runtimes may remain where useful; technical separation does not imply constitutional independence.

## Ownership convergence target

| Existing repository / identity | v0.5 responsibility owner | Target meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `MOMO-Observatory` | **MOMO** | Its System semantic-source/observation capability is absorbed into MOMO responsibility. It does not remain a separate responsibility-bearing Actor. |
| `references` | **MOMO** | Reusable, noncanonical knowledge becomes MOMO-owned System knowledge/evidence. Reference remains evidence, not authority. |
| `Dango` | **INNU** | Model/tier routing, prompt compilation and model-selection learning become part of INNU's capability-realization/model-mastery responsibility. |
| `FROG` | **SARU** | Reality Mentor / Leap capability becomes part of SARU's Human World Activation and reality-experimentation responsibility. |
| `SandFrog` | **SARU** | Experimental substrate may remain technically separate/disposable, but its responsibility belongs to SARU. |

Historical repositories remain provenance until separately retired. Routine v0.5 recovery should ultimately not require MOMO-Observatory, references, Dango or FROG as independent current authorities.

## MOMO source responsibility in v0.5

MOMO becomes the accountable System home for:

- Human-ratified System Purpose and Principles;
- System-wide semantic relationships and current integrated interpretation;
- Human-facing views / Cockpit meaning;
- reusable System knowledge and evidence intended for cross-Application use;
- provenance linking current meaning to historical sources and Human decisions.

This does not make MOMO authoritative over another Application's original facts or internals. The owning Application remains authoritative for its own responsibility, implementation and outward facts. MOMO may represent and relate those facts; it must not silently overwrite them.

## Human authority

Human is outside the five-Actor count: Human is the source of Purpose and final constitutional authority, not an Application responsibility inside the System.

Human alone may finally ratify constitutional additions, changes or retirement. AI may recover, interpret, propose, review and implement within delegation; it may not self-ratify constitutional meaning.

## KIBI remains connection, not control

KIBI owns semantic connection across independent Applications: identity, versioned semantic Contract, scoped/revocable authority context, capability/knowledge distinction, lifecycle/compatibility meaning, durable handoff/provenance, and first-class negative/recovery states.

KIBI does not choose another Application's internal work, own its task graph, become the central runtime, or become a Supervisor.

## KIJI / INNU / SARU working responsibilities

**KIJI → Requirements**  
Observe, research, interpret, quantify and reduce uncertainty until evidence-backed, implementable Requirements exist.

**INNU → Product / Capability**  
Choose and master the implementation/model/tool path, build the accepted Product/Capability and finish it to the required evidence standard.

**SARU → Human World Activation**  
Turn accepted Product/Capability into authorized operation, adoption, experiments and real-world state change while respecting Human authority and external-mutation boundaries.

They may reuse one another's capability and learning without erasing responsibility boundaries.

## Reference is evidence, not authority

Reusable references, research, model-routing episodes, experiment reports and historical artifacts are evidence inputs. They do not become constitutional or operational authority merely because they are newer, detailed, machine-readable or stored under MOMO/INNU/SARU.

Current interpretation must preserve distinctions among fact, observation, interpretation, representation/view, evidence, currentness, authority and history.

## Historical retirement principle

A repository whose responsibility is absorbed may later become historical/read-only/retired only after migration and recovery evidence prove that the current System no longer depends on it as a live authority. Retirement preserves provenance and accepted historical evidence; retirement is not deletion of history.

No retirement is activated by this Phase 1 candidate.

## Phase 1 non-goals

This candidate does not:

- activate v0.5;
- replace current `PRINCIPLES.md` or `SOURCE_AND_VIEWS.md`;
- migrate repository contents or runtime state;
- change KIBI Protocol implementation;
- change KIJI, INNU or SARU runtime behavior;
- move secrets, credentials or private evidence;
- retire repositories;
- start autonomous cycles;
- authorize external mutation;
- infer Phase 2 authority from Phase 1 completion.

## Activation condition

This candidate can become Current constitutional meaning only through an explicit later Human-authorized activation after the applicable migration, semantic, recovery and review gates.

Until then:

> **Current remains v0. Human-approved v0.5 direction is recorded here as an inactive destination, not as already-completed reality.**
