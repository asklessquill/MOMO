# MOMO System v0.5 — Phase 9 Historical Retirement Readiness

Status: **DRAFT / NO RETIREMENT AUTHORITY / DO NOT EXECUTE BEFORE ACTIVATION + FRESH RECOVERY + FINAL REVIEW**

Purpose: decide whether formerly independent responsibility repositories can be treated as historical or owner-controlled technical modules without harming recovery, provenance, privacy or capability.

Retirement is not required merely to make the Actor count five. Responsibility convergence and physical repository retirement are separate decisions.

## Global retirement criteria

A source repository is retirement-ready only when all are true:

1. activated v0.5 Current meaning no longer depends on it as an independent authority;
2. Phase 8 Fresh Actor recovery succeeds without resurrecting it as an Actor;
3. required capabilities/evidence remain reachable through the responsible owner or a declared owner-controlled module/archive path;
4. provenance and historical acceptance evidence remain attributable;
5. privacy/classification is preserved or improved;
6. recovery procedures do not silently require its old default branch or mutable `main`;
7. no current runtime or connector depends on an undeclared path in that repository;
8. no active Human commission names it as an independent responsibility owner;
9. rollback/recovery refs and archives are adequate before any destructive action;
10. final independent Ultra review has not identified a reason to keep independent authority semantics.

If a repository is technically useful after responsibility convergence, prefer **module/archive status** over deletion.

## Retirement actions are separately bounded

Potential actions include labeling historical status, archiving, making read-only, transferring module ownership, moving selected content, or eventually deleting obsolete technical surfaces.

Each destructive action requires its own evidence and Human authority. Do not batch destructive retirement merely because semantic v0.5 activation succeeded.

## Closeout condition

Phase 9 can close when every absorbed repository has an explicit disposition:

- retained owner-controlled module;
- archived historical evidence;
- physically merged with provenance preserved; or
- safely deleted after independent recovery proof.

The closeout receipt must identify the disposition and exact durable recovery location for each source.
