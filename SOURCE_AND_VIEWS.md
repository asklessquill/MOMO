# One semantic truth, multiple views

> **MOMO-Observatory semantic truth → MOMO Human-facing projection**

This is an ownership and semantic relationship, not a central runtime or a synchronization architecture.

## Ownership and authority

| Meaning | Accountable source | Views and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Human Purpose, Direction and constitutional decisions | Human; explicit Human provenance retained in MOMO-Observatory | MOMO represents the recorded decision. Neither repository substitutes for Human or turns AI interpretation into a Human decision. |
| MOMO System-wide Purpose/Principles, semantic relationships and integrated interpretation | MOMO-Observatory | MOMO provides authorized Human-facing explanation, principles, navigation and visualizations. A projection has no independent constitutional editing authority. |
| KIBI semantic connection Principles and Protocol meaning | KIBI, under Human constitutional authority | MOMO-Observatory references KIBI; MOMO explains the connection boundary. It does not maintain a competing KIBI Contract. |
| Application responsibility, implementation and original outward facts | The owning Application and its authorized sources | Observatory may hold outward state and a sourced interpretation. It does not override source facts, own internals or direct internal work. |

MOMO-Observatory is the **Actor / AI-readable semantic source for MOMO System meaning**. It may contain structured Purpose, Principles, Human Direction, System-wide semantic state, Application outward state, provenance, evidence references and Human decision boundaries. The repository's source role is scoped to MOMO meaning; it does not centralize every Application's truth or turn observation into authority.

MOMO is the **Human-facing projection of that same semantic truth**. Public explanation, Human-native semantic compression, Pages/Cockpit experiences, visualizations, readable Principles and navigation are views. A public view can be a deliberately limited subset. Private Human views may show additional authorized meaning.

MOMO is not Human. MOMO-Observatory is not a Supervisor. Human alone finally sets Purpose and constitutional meaning. Source repositories, projections, credentials, model outputs and old permissions do not manufacture current authority.

## Meaning that must survive a view

A view must preserve the source identity and revision, its semantic scope, Human provenance where applicable, decision/ratification status, and the distinction between fact, inference, proposal and unknown. Human-scale summaries may omit detail but must not reverse meaning or conceal decision-relevant limitations.

The authoritative record of a Human Direction distinguishes original Human words from AI transcription, interpretation and derived organization. A dated record identifies the decision and approved scope. Review and publication evidence remain separate from the Human decision; a Git author name or successful push does not prove Human approval.

For Application observations, preserve the original source/revision and observation limits. An integrated interpretation can be authoritative as MOMO's current interpretation without becoming authoritative over an Application's actual state. When source facts conflict or cannot be checked, preserve the conflict or unknown and the relevant source ownership.

## Change, disagreement and recovery

Semantic changes are recorded at the accountable source under applicable authority before dependent views are refreshed. An edit in a Human-facing view that changes Purpose or Principles must be captured as Human Direction or a proposal with provenance; it cannot silently create a second Canonical version. Presentation-only changes may proceed within existing delegation.

If views disagree, compare their source revisions, semantic scope and authority evidence. A newer-looking page does not overrule the source. Mark stale, partial, unavailable or unresolved content and correct the affected view. Do not merge incompatible claims merely to make displays agree.

If the private semantic source is unavailable to a reader, an authorized versioned public projection may convey the published meaning and its provenance. It does not certify unobserved newer state or grant private access. Actors use previously verified material only within applicable existing authority; they must not invent freshness, an offline grace period or missing Human decisions.

## Privacy and publication

The repository boundary is also an access boundary. MOMO-Observatory is private; MOMO is public. Source status does not authorize publication of everything in the source.

Publish only the authorized public-safe subset. This Task authorizes public System philosophy, the responsibility transition and the source/view relationship. It does not authorize export of private Application state, raw evidence, tenant information, credentials, confidential payloads or internal logs. Evidence references retain their access control. An omitted private item must not be turned into a false public claim of absence or success.

Reusable capability and learning may cross outward boundaries when useful and authorized. Sharing requires enough meaning, scope and limitations to be used responsibly; it does not imply unrestricted publication, a common internal database, or responsibility transfer.

## Current document realization and remaining design

The Canonical MOMO meaning is held in `semantic/PURPOSE_AND_PRINCIPLES.md` and this document, with the Human record in `governance/decisions/2026-09-05-responsibility-transition.md`. MOMO publishes attributed Human-facing projections, with exact source revision and content digest where text is reproduced. The digest supports correspondence checking; it is not a Human signature or an access grant.

Existing `state/*.json` and the observation schema are provisional snapshots with their own recorded observation times. They are not Canonical Principles or fresh observations merely because this repository has acquired a semantic-source role. Application sources remain authoritative for their outward facts. The old private GitHub Project is a Human view; this transition does not update it or claim it synchronized.

Canonical: the single-source/multiple-view relationship, source ownership, provenance, privacy and authority boundaries. Lower-level and deferred: schemas, storage layout as a permanent standard, projection generation/synchronization, editing workflow mechanics, conflict-resolution software, public release automation, the full MOMO Cockpit architecture and the full KIBI Protocol. This Task implements the documents and their clear source references only, with no runtime or Application migration.
