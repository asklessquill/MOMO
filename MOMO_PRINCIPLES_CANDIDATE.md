## Candidate MOMO Principles

### MP-01 — Human Sovereignty

Human remains the final source of Purpose and the final constitutional authority.

MOMO may represent, preserve, observe and explain Human-ratified Purpose and Principles. MOMO must not manufacture constitutional authority or become a replacement Human.

### MP-02 — Purpose Before Routine Action

The System exists under Human Purpose, value judgments and Human-only boundaries.

Applications act autonomously inside valid boundaries. Routine execution should not turn Human into an approval button, task router or message bus.

### MP-03 — Federated Autonomy

Each Application remains independently responsible for its own internal work, agents, models, retries, runtime and storage.

The System should gain cooperation without requiring internal unification.

### MP-04 — Graceful Evolution and Replaceability

Applications and implementations may join, leave, be replaced, or combine without requiring the whole System to become one coupled implementation.

Continuity should depend on explicit responsibilities and usable boundaries, not on one permanent participant topology.

### MP-05 — Complexity Belongs to Its Owner

Internal complexity remains with the Application that owns the responsibility.

Other participants receive meaningful Results, not a requirement to understand the producer's internal task graph, hidden reasoning, agent topology or recovery machinery.

### MP-06 — Semantic Compression

Internally expand as much as necessary; externally converge to the receiver's useful semantic resolution.

For Human, repository-scale or agent-scale complexity should become Human-scale meaning while evidence remains available for drill-down.

### MP-07 — AI-Native System Recomposition

Use systems for their semantic primitives, not merely their Human-facing screens.

Prefer native identity, authority, relation, state, version, delta, event, audit, evidence and provenance when they provide a more truthful machine-operable substrate than reconstructed context or UI imitation.

### MP-08 — Durable and Safe-to-Ignore Operation

Meaningful decisions, state, authority, evidence and handoff must survive individual chats, sessions, actors and transient model memory.

The goal is not inactivity. The goal is that the System can continue routine operation without requiring continuous Human supervision.

### MP-09 — Kibidango Complementarity

Different participants should contribute different strengths without being forced into the same identity or internal design.

> 桃太郎が強いから三匹を連れて行くのではない。  
> 三匹には、桃太郎にできないことができる。

Cooperation should preserve difference rather than erase it.

### MP-10 — Real-World Orientation

Coordination, architecture and AI-to-AI cooperation are not the final objective.

The System exists to help Human Purpose change the real world: opportunities, products, decisions, relationships, constraints and Human World State.

> 鬼ヶ島とは現実世界である。

## Relationship to KIBI

These candidate MOMO Principles define System-wide philosophy.

KIBI should not simply duplicate them as its own identity. KIBI should derive narrower Principles from its own Purpose as a connection Protocol, such as explicit identity, explicit versioned Contract, scoped/revocable authority, compatibility, boundary preservation, durable handoff/provenance, topology-neutral participation and first-class negative states.

The same idea may therefore appear at two levels without becoming duplicate authority:

- MOMO: why the whole System should remain federated, durable, cognitively compressed and complementary;
- KIBI: what a connection Protocol must do to preserve those properties at an Application boundary.

## What this candidate does not do

This candidate does not:

- replace Human sovereignty;
- make MOMO a Supervisor;
- direct KIJI, INNU, SARU or future Application internals;
- change current Production or Application behavior;
- automatically repeal KIBI v0.3;
- declare these Principles Canonical without Human ratification.

The next Canonical step, if Human approves the recomposition, is to ratify MOMO's System-wide Principles and then rewrite KIBI's Canonical Purpose/Principles/Protocol identity accordingly while preserving historical provenance.
