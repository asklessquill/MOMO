What actually happened

The order matters. KIBI did not lose its identity. It found it.

1

KIBI asked what it exists to do

Not “what files should move?” Not “how do we separate MOMO?” The first question was the Purpose of KIBI itself.

2

KIBI defined its Purpose

Enable different autonomous Applications to connect and cooperate safely and meaningfully without losing their independence.

3

A mismatch became visible

KIBI had been carrying two kinds of things: System-wide philosophy and connection-protocol rules. Both had grown there, but they did not belong at the same level forever.

4

The philosophy was handed to MOMO

Human sovereignty, federated autonomy, semantic compression, AI-native recomposition, complementarity and other System-wide ideas became MOMO Principle candidates.

5

KIBI re-derived its own Principles

With its Purpose clear, KIBI rebuilt its Principles from first principles — then derived Protocol methodology from them.

The handoff

KIBI after the clarification

KIBI does not become smaller. It becomes sharper.

KIBI Purpose

異なる自律Applicationが、それぞれの独立性を失わず、安全かつ意味的に接続・協働できる状態を成立させること。

KIBI is the Protocol that makes connection possible.

KP-01 · Preserve independent participation

A connection must not take ownership of an Application's internals or independence.

KP-02 · Establish explicit shared meaning

Participants need an explicit agreement about meaning, obligations and compatibility.

KP-03 · Act only under applicable current authority

Past permission, identity or successful connection is never enough by itself to authorize a current effect.

KP-04 · Preserve truthful, recoverable boundary state

A fresh Actor must be able to recover what is agreed, observed, unresolved and still permitted.
